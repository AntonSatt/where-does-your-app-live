Where does live?

Type a domain or paste a URL. Or try hetzner.com, svt.se or github.com.

How it works

  1. Follow the name. DNS shows the aliases and addresses behind the domain. The aliases often name the platform outright, like cloudfront.net or vercel-dns.com.
  2. Find the owner. Every address belongs to a network. The RIPE NCC's public data says which network and who holds it, and a hand-checked list maps the big networks to their parent company.
  3. Judge by owner, not by place. A US company falls under the CLOUD Act wherever its servers stand. A CDN in front decrypts the traffic and hides the server behind it, so the answer says that instead of guessing.

A best-effort technical check, not legal advice. Data from RIPEstat (RIPE NCC), GeoLite2 by MaxMind via RIPEstat, and the IP ranges AWS and Google Cloud publish. This page loads nothing from third parties. Source on GitHub.
